# Portfolio

A selection of the projects where I bring together data, analytics and coaching. Some are finished pieces of work, others are tools I keep building and using with my teams.

### [Derby della Capitale, by the numbers](/about_me/Derby_Roma_2026.html)
Data preview of Maxima Roma – BC Roma (LBA 2026-27, 11 October 2026), built on the league play-by-play and the EuroCup box scores. Also in [italiano](/about_me/Derby_Roma_2026_it.html). The pipeline behind it, [lba-2627-data](https://github.com/martinig94/lba-2627-data), collects every LBA game weekly and regenerates game, team, matchup and player reports in one command.

### [Fenerbahce 2025-2026 Scouting Report](https://martinig94.github.io/basketball_analytics/)
Scouting report on Fenerbahce's 2025-2026 season, prepared for the EHCB Euroleague Coaches Academy.

### [GBP Girls Summer Challenge](https://gbp-girls.vercel.app/#/challenge)
Summer activities and challenges for my U13 women's team at GBP Frassati Roma.

### [Basketball Practice Planner](https://bball-practice-planner.streamlit.app/)
A practice planning buddy for coaches. Work in progress.
